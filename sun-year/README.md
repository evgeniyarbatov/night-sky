# sunset-sunrise-azimuth

Where does the Sun rise and set throughout the year? Another project that came to mind during a long morning run to Yen So.

I am interested in learning patterns of how daylight changes, the patterns that are too hard to see because changes happen gradually and over a long period of time.

The sun affects my photography, what I see on my early morning and late evening runs, when I wake up and what kind of ideas come to my mind so it makes to study it.

The sun is also one of the easiest astronomical objects to observe. It is an invitation to study the stars that are far away from the Earth.
